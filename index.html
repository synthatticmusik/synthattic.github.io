01 / THE ARTISTPROFILE

A sound built
from contrast.

SYNTHATTIC is the sonic luminary and musical alter ego of Ashutosh Purohit, an independent producer and DJ moving between cinematic soundscapes, intricate drums, warm nostalgic textures and rhythm-led electronic music.

His sound crosses atmospheric electronica, progressive and melodic/organic house, Afro-inspired club rhythms and techno, balancing introspection with the physical energy of the dance floor. Years of experimentation have shaped a genre-open approach: emotional, kinetic and built around contrast.

His project D.I.Y is a deeply personal body of work dedicated to Snoop, his late Persian cat and studio companion. It reflects persistence, rebuilding and the decision to make music on his own terms.
